How our data solutions work

One connected flow, from data sources to decisions.

Every Alera Prime solution follows the same architecture: data is integrated once, modeled consistently, and reused across reporting and analytics.

  1. 1

    Sources

    Relevant business data is identified across ERP, CRM, finance, operational systems, files, and cloud applications.

    ResultSource inventory

  2. 2

    Integration

    Data is extracted, cleaned, combined, and validated on a reliable schedule.

    ResultIntegrated, quality-checked data

  3. 3

    Data model

    Data is organized into a documented model with agreed business rules and KPI logic.

    ResultOne consistent data foundation

  4. 4

    Analytics & reporting

    Reports, KPI monitoring, and analysis are built on top of the model.

    ResultReporting and insight per area

  5. 5

    Decisions

    Management works from consistent, timely information.

    ResultAligned, informed decisions

Our working method

A structured five-step method. Clear outputs at every stage.

Each step ends with a defined output, so progress is visible and decisions are made together.

  1. 1

    Requirements

    Stakeholder interviews to agree business goals, key questions, and KPI and reporting needs.

    OutputRequirements summary

  2. 2

    Data assessment

    A review of source systems, data quality, and current reporting.

    OutputAssessment findings

  3. 3

    Solution planning

    Architecture and data model design, with an agreed scope, priorities, phasing, and timeline.

    OutputSolution plan

  4. 4

    Development & validation

    Staged development, reconciled with source data and accepted with your team.

    OutputValidated solution

  5. 5

    Ongoing support

    Monitoring and maintenance, enhancements on request, and user support.

    OutputSupport plan, where agreed

FAQ

Common questions

Anything else is best covered in a short introductory meeting.

Where does Alera Prime work?

Alera Prime works with organizations in the United States and Europe.

Can we engage just one service?

Yes. Each service can be engaged on its own or combined into a complete data solution. Scope and priorities are always agreed with you before work begins.

Who delivers the work?

Our own team, following a structured five-step method: requirements, data assessment, solution planning, development and validation, and ongoing support.

How do you make sure the numbers are right?

Figures are reconciled with source systems before go-live, and the solution is accepted together with your team. Data models, logic, and processes are documented for your team.

What happens in the introductory meeting?

A 15–20 minute conversation about your current data and reporting setup, the reporting and analysis needs that matter most to you, and which services fit and how an engagement could start.
