FAQ

Common questions

Anything else is best covered in a short introductory meeting.

Where does Alera Prime work?

Alera Prime works with organizations in the United States and Europe.

Can we engage just one service?

Yes. Each service can be engaged on its own or combined into a complete data solution. Scope and priorities are always agreed with you before work begins.

Who delivers the work?

Our own team, following a structured five-step method: requirements, data assessment, solution planning, development and validation, and ongoing support.

How do you make sure the numbers are right?

Figures are reconciled with source systems before go-live, and the solution is accepted together with your team. Data models, logic, and processes are documented for your team.

Are the companies listed under contractor experience your clients?

They are organizations where Alera Prime has worked in a contractor capacity, not direct Alera Prime clients. We are happy to discuss the nature of this work during an introductory meeting.

What happens in the introductory meeting?

A 15–20 minute conversation about your current data and reporting setup, the reporting and analysis needs that matter most to you, and which services fit and how an engagement could start.
