The challenge

Most companies don’t lack data. They lack data they can rely on.

We fix the foundations first and build the reporting on top, so the answer to “what was revenue last quarter?” is the same whoever asks.

01

The numbers don’t match

Finance, sales and operations report different figures for the same metric, and meetings turn into debates about whose spreadsheet is right.

02

Reporting eats the week

Analysts spend days exporting, cleaning and pasting data together instead of analyzing it — and the report is out of date by the time it lands.

03

Dashboards nobody opens

Reports were built around what the tool could show, not the questions leadership actually asks. Trust drops, and people go back to Excel.

FAQ

Questions clients ask

Something else on your mind? Send us a message and we’ll answer directly.

What size of company do you work with?

Mostly growing and mid-sized companies that have outgrown spreadsheets or a first BI setup, and individual departments within larger organizations that need focused help.

Which BI tools do you recommend?

The one that fits your team, budget and existing systems. We work with Power BI, Tableau, Looker and the major cloud platforms, and we don’t resell licenses, so our advice stays neutral. If your current tools can do the job, we’ll say so.

Can you work alongside our internal data team?

Yes. Many engagements are a mix: we take on the architecture or the heavy lifting, your team works with us throughout, and we finish with training and documentation so they own the result.

How do you handle data security and privacy?

We sign an NDA before seeing any data, work with least-privilege access and inside your environment wherever possible, and sign a data processing agreement where required. Our designs account for GDPR and CCPA from the start.

How is pricing structured?

Assessments are fixed price. Projects are priced by fixed scope or milestone, and ongoing support is a monthly retainer. After a first call you receive a written proposal with scope, timeline and cost.

Do you work with clients in both the US and Europe?

Yes. We run engagements remotely with on-site workshops when they add value, and schedule our working hours to overlap with your team, whether you’re in New York, Chicago, London or Berlin.
