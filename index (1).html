01Who we are

Alera Prime (“Alera Prime”, “we”, “us”) is a Data & Business Intelligence services company working with organizations in the United States and Europe. We are responsible for the personal information described in this notice.

This notice covers this website (aleraprime.com) and enquiries you send to us. It does not cover data that we process on behalf of clients as part of a services engagement; that processing is governed by the agreement with the relevant client.

02Information we collect

When you visit this website

You can browse this website without telling us who you are. The site has no user accounts or sign-up forms, and we do not ask you to submit personal information through it.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with most websites, the hosting provider receives technical information needed to deliver pages to your browser. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor’s IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. For details, please see GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to share, such as:

  • your name and email address;
  • your company and role, if you include them;
  • the content of your message and any further correspondence.

Please do not send us sensitive personal information by email unless we have agreed a secure way to share it.

03How we use information

We use the information you send us to:

  • reply to your enquiry and answer your questions;
  • arrange and hold an introductory meeting;
  • discuss our services and, where requested, prepare a proposal;
  • keep a record of our correspondence with you.

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, we rely on our legitimate interests in responding to business enquiries and, where relevant, on taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.

04Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, or other tracking technologies. Fonts and images are served from this website itself rather than from third-party services.

The website links to external sites, such as our LinkedIn page. If you follow those links, the other site’s own privacy and cookie policies apply.

If we introduce cookies or analytics in the future, we will update this notice and, where required by law, ask for your consent first.

05Sharing information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising purposes.

Service providers that host this website and provide our email service process information on our behalf so that we can operate the website and communicate with you. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

06International transfers

Because we work with organizations in the United States and Europe, and because our service providers may operate in different countries, your information may be processed outside the country where you live. Where data protection law requires it, we take steps to make sure that your information remains protected when it is transferred.

07How long we keep it

We keep enquiry correspondence only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this notice, including any resulting business relationship, or as required by law. When it is no longer needed, we delete it.

08Data protection

We take appropriate measures to protect personal information against loss, misuse, and unauthorized access. This website is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and it does not collect personal information through forms.

No method of transmitting or storing information is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you have a concern, please contact us.

09Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights regarding your personal information:

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom

  • Access — to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • Rectification — to ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • Erasure — to ask us to delete your information;
  • Restriction — to ask us to limit how we use your information;
  • Objection — to object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • Portability — to receive information you provided to us in a commonly used format;
  • Complaint — to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

If you are in the United States

Residents of certain US states, such as California, may have the right to know what personal information we hold about them, to request access to it, to correct it, and to request its deletion. We do not sell personal information and will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

How to exercise your rights

To make a request, email partnerships@aleraprime.com. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on your request, and we will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.

10Children

This website and our services are intended for businesses and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
