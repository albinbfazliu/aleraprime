Privacy notice

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This notice explains how Aleraprime (“we”, “us”) handles personal data you share with us through aleraprime.com.

What we collect

When you use our contact form we collect the details you enter: your name, work email, company, role, region, the topic you select and your message. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies on this website.

Why we use it

We use these details only to respond to your enquiry, arrange a call and, where relevant, prepare a proposal. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries, or steps taken at your request before entering into a contract.

Who processes it

Form submissions are delivered to us by Formspree, which acts as our processor. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request and any resulting business relationship, and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Your rights

Depending on where you live — including under the GDPR in Europe and the CCPA in California — you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. To make a request, email partnerships@aleraprime.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Contact

Questions about this notice can be sent to partnerships@aleraprime.com.
